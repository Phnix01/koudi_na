Where did your money go this month? Koudi Na shows you at a glance: what came in, what went out, and what is left.

"Kudi" means money in Hausa. Koudi Na is a pocket cashbook, designed in Niamey for everyday life: morning bread paid in cash, rent paid from the bank, phone credit topped up with mobile money, a savings-club contribution, the money you set aside for later.

LOG IT WITHOUT THE HASSLE
• Tap +, then pick Expense or Income. Long-press + to go straight to a new expense.
• One screen, no scrolling: the amount on the keypad, the category from the grid, the account.
• Every category keeps its place, and your last category and account are already selected.
• Add a note or change the date when you need to. Made a mistake? Edit the entry, or undo a deletion right away.
• Payments that come back every month (rent, a subscription, a savings club) are created for you.

YOUR MONTH ON ONE SCREEN
• Your monthly balance, your spending pace and an estimate of where the month will end.
• Your average spending per day.
• Your latest entries, and a quick switch from one month to the next.

THE 40/25/35 RULE
Each month, your income is split into three envelopes: 40% Savings, 25% Family, 35% Living. For each one you see what was planned, what was spent and what is left. An alert lets you know when an envelope goes over.

CASH, BANK, MOBILE MONEY, VAULT
Four starter accounts, each with its own balance. Add your own, rename them, change their icon and colour. Your categories can be customised too. Koudi Na is not connected to any bank or mobile operator: you log the numbers, and the app never touches your money.

THE JOURNAL
Every entry, day by day. Search by note, category, account or amount, and filter by account or category.

INSIGHTS
• Month, 6 months or year: income and spending side by side.
• Where the money goes: your biggest categories and their share.
• Your savings path against your goal.
• The days of the week that cost you the most.
• Short notes such as "Your Food spending is up 32% this month", plus a money tip every day.

STAY ON TRACK
A streak counts the days you log at least one entry, and badges mark your progress. A daily reminder at the time you choose and a weekly recap, all of which can be turned off.

150+ CURRENCIES
CFA franc (XOF, XAF), naira, cedi, euro, dollar, pound, Swiss franc, Canadian dollar, dirham and many more.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
Koudi Na works 100% offline: no sign-up, no account, no server, no ads, no trackers. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to export your data as CSV, PDF or Excel. You can also import a CSV file.

The app interface is available in English and French.

Koudi Na is made in Niger by XNOVA Systems.
